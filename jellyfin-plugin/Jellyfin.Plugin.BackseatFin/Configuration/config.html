BackseatFin

Plays Jellyfin in the car's browser on a canvas, which cars don't block while driving. For passengers: the driver must keep their eyes on the road.

Optional. Hostnames that serve Jellyfin to the car only (point an extra hostname at Jellyfin in your reverse proxy). Every browser that opens one of them gets the car player. Leave empty to switch each car browser on by itself instead: open /BackseatFin/car in the car and turn car mode on. Other browsers keep Jellyfin's own player.
